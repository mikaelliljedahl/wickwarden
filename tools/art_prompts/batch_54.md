The three variants of a unit differ in composition and detail only, never in design. Walk sheets are drawn side first (the accepted side sheet is the design reference for the down and up sheets); action sheets name the walk sheet of their own facing as their design reference (see each reference line). Producer f (batches 48 to 59), wording and facing rules from `tools/art_prompts/PROGRESS.md` and `PROGRESS_a.md`; invented details in `DESIGNS_f.md`.

Shared rules used in every entry: the shared block of `art-brief.md`, then the sheet block (or the light block for radial light textures, written from the sheet block with no figure), the facing sentence for each figure, the layout line and `Image size`.

Rows: `hollow_captain_walk`: 9, `hollow_captain_actions`: 6.
