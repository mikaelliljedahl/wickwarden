Original game art for "Wickwarden", a painterly top-down adventure. Hand-painted storybook illustration style with visible brush strokes and soft edges, high detail, warm lamp light against deep dusk colours, low saturation except for light sources. NOT pixel art, no pixel grid, no dithering, no thick black outlines. Three-quarter top-down view, camera about 50 degrees above the ground, key light from the upper left. Exactly 2 separate poses in one horizontal row, both the same size and on one shared ground line. A small round brass-rimmed socket the size of a coin, a short brass hinge at the top, and a dark recess in the middle. Pose 1 empty: the recess holds only deep red glass with a dark core that is dark and cold, with no light. Pose 2 lit: the same socket with the recess filled with a strong red-orange, a soft glow spreading a little beyond the rim. Seen from the front-top at a three-quarter angle. Softer edges and a little more wear on the brass rim. The design, outfit and proportions are identical in every pose. No extra poses, no cropping, no touching. Painterly high detail, no pixel art, no text. Image size: 1536x1024.
```
