```text
Original game art for "Wickwarden", a painterly top-down adventure. Exactly 1 separate pose in one horizontal row on a 1536x1024 landscape canvas, all at the same scale, all bases on one common ground line, wide equal gaps. Pose 1, one square tile of straight boardwalk: four weathered grey planks running left to right with dark gaps between them, two iron nail heads on each plank, the thin front edge of the planks visible at the bottom. Seen from above at an angle, a single 96-pixel tile. Variation for this image: Neutral, clear composition; poses evenly spaced and well separated, the silhouette plain and readable. No extra poses, no cropping, no touching, no text. Painterly high detail, no pixel art.

```
