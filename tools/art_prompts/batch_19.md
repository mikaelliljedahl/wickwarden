```text
Original game art for "Wickwarden", a painterly top-down adventure. Hand-painted storybook illustration style with visible brush strokes and soft edges, high detail, warm lamp light against deep dusk colours, low saturation except for light sources. NOT pixel art, no pixel grid, no dithering, no thick black outlines. Three-quarter top-down view, camera about 50 degrees above the ground, key light from the upper left. Poses in one row stand on one shared invisible ground line and have the same scale. The figure is identical in every pose: same design, same colours, same proportions.

A world pickup: the tallow sling, a short forked dark-wood handle with two leather bands and a pouch resting on a short carved stone pedestal with a worn lantern motif (no text). The pedestal is the same in every pose. Exactly 4 separate poses in 2 horizontal rows on a 1536x1024 landscape canvas, top row first, with a clear empty gap between the rows; all poses at the same scale, each row on one baseline, wide equal gaps inside each row. Row 1 (1 poses): idle: the object still on the pedestal. Row 2 (3 poses): glint: the same object with a small bright sparkle moving across its surface, three steps.

Variation for this image: Neutral, clear composition.

Image size: 1536x1024.
```
