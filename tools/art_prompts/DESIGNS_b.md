# Invented design details, art batches 12 to 19

The art brief does not describe these. Each line is what the prompts of this range say, so that every
variant of one image uses the same design. Tamsin's design is not changed here.

## Batch 12: shared cave sets (textures)

- Set "stone" (pale cut stone and polished marble): fitted pale slabs with fine grey veining and dark thin joints; wall faces in even horizontal courses. Neutral value range so code can tint it.
- Set "root" (root, earth and wax rock): packed dark earth with thin pale roots, wax drips and wax-hard earth; wall tops are root tangles; faces are vertical roots with wax runs. Neutral value range.
- Observed (not a prompt change): the stone and root textures came back rubble-heavy and warm-toned in most variants (see tools/source_art/log_b12.md). No prompt wording was changed for batch 12.

## Batch 13: cave water, cave edges, Hush creatures

- Black cave water: very dark violet-black, with faint lamp reflections only (the prompt asks for no floating objects). The generator drew warm orange reflections; logged as weak, no wording change.
- Cave edge strips: the opaque rock or water band at the top of the strip, fading to the key at the bottom. The tool returned about 2.5:1 (1983x793, 2048x768), not the 4:1 of the strip rule (384x96 cell); see the known problems of PROGRESS_b.md.
- Bramble snapper: a thorn-root with a wide jaw of hawthorn spikes and a red throat, leafy pale-green tufts at the base, rooted, no legs; faces right in three-quarter view.
- Fen leech: a long wax-black leech with ribbed segments, a blunt round head with dim eyes and a pale lamp-wick tail that glows when it surfaces. Rows: surface 3, idle surfaced 2, telegraph 2, lunge 2, dive 3.
- Cinder wisp: a round ember core with a smoky tail, two dim eyes in the glow and sparks around it; float 4 and swell 2 (swell is larger and whiter).

## Batch 14: watcher eye, husk hound walk

- Watcher eye: a carved stone eye socket in a square mount of dark ruined stone with moss, a heavy stone lid, a cold blue iris. Rows: lid closed 1, opening 3, open 1, fire 2 (a thin pale-blue bolt). Side view looks right; down view faces the viewer; up view shows the back.
- Husk hound: a thin grey hound from sacks, rope and rusted lantern parts; a rusted lantern cage on its back with no flame; rope tail; cold teal glow in the long head.

## Batch 15: husk hound states, brine crab, wickling

- Brine crab: a barnacled crab with a plate shell like a rusted breastplate (teal-grey and rust), pale barnacles on the edges, six thin legs, eye stalks with small dark tips, and one huge claw (the claw is on the right side in the side view). Sidestep 4 poses; claws up; two lunges; guard spark (a short bright spark on the plate).
- Wickling: a thumb-high candle imp: stub of pale cream wax, two tiny legs, small bright orange flame head, two dim arms. Run 4 and pause 1 in one direction.
- Husk states: crouch, charge (2) and dizzy (2) of the batch 14 hound, side only.
