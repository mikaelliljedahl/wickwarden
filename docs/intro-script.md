# Wickwarden intro script

## Still 1: intro_01_lanterns
Nine great lights drift slowly above the valley of Vey.
Each one keeps a thing alive: warmth, rain, song and trade.
The village sleeps under them, and the valley is calm.
(hold: 8)

## Still 2: intro_02_lanternfall
One night, all nine lanterns fall from the sky.
They do not break. They burn down into the ground.
Where each one lands, a crater, a ruin or a cave glows.
(hold: 8)

## Still 3: intro_03_dusk
Since that night, the valley lives in a dusk that does not end.
Out of the dusk come the Hush: forgotten songs and lost tools.
They are not cruel. They are hungry, and they want only light.
(hold: 8)

## Still 4: intro_04_tamsin
In Brackwater Ford, Tamsin is an apprentice lamplighter.
She lights the street lamps at night and trims the wicks by morning.
(hold: 6)

## Still 5: intro_05_hook
Her master, Old Corrow, was the last Wickwarden.
He is gone. He left his lamp hook and a lamp that will not go out.
Tamsin takes them both and keeps them close.
(hold: 7)

## Still 6: intro_06_setout
Tamsin walks the village path with the hook in one hand.
Ahead, a cave mouth glows. Somewhere below, a fallen lantern burns.
She sets out to find it and light it again.
(hold: 8)
